# Club crests

The two clubs are fictional (AGENTS.md: synthetic data only). Their crests were generated by Kian
Lakhani with Google Gemini (Oct 24, 2026), chosen by him, and traced here into flat-colour SVG
without redesign: each pixel took the nearest of the crest's own colours, each colour region was
vectorised (OpenCV contours, simplified to within 0.6 px at 4x), and the layers were stacked in
drawing order. They show no real club's mark and no city's coat of arms.

- `london-athletic.svg`: London Athletic, home. Navy shield, off-white field, gold star, the river
  through a low-poly navy hill.
- `lisbon-esportes.svg`: Lisbon Esportes, away. Gold and off-white roundel with a tram.

Google's terms for Gemini leave generated content with the person who generated it; we use these as
the project's own artwork. Checked at 24, 32 and 150 px on the dark app.
