# Challenge map

Every requirement in the challenge brief, and where The Ninety Labs answers it. Keep this file honest:
mark a row `planned` until it runs in the demo, and update a row when it ships. Milestones are
defined in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md#milestones).

Status words: **built** (merged on `main`, with a command or test, and where there is a number, a
committed evidence file); **partial** (part of the row is built; the row says which part, and what
is in an open pull request or not built); **planned** (not built). A number not in a committed
file is written **not measured** or **pending**, never quoted. Audited against `main` at
`ea7743a8` on Oct 25, 2026.

**The engine is frozen** at `65247ba3` (#276): every demo and freeze measurement is on runs from
that SHA (`runs/f<seed>-65247ba3`). **The demo match is seed 8080**, a 4-1 condensed to 16.9
minutes, chosen by Kian after seeing the pre-registered rule's result, including the goal timings;
the rule's own pick, seed 8076, and its full table are recorded ([demo-match.md](eval/demo-match.md),
#296, #305). Known engine gaps at the freeze, not fixed before the deadline: too much sideways
recycling and too little build-up (ranked first in [defect-sweep-7ee8720.md](eval/defect-sweep-7ee8720.md),
measured on an older engine); long shots taken through no defender (84% to 87% of long shots had
nobody screening the goal, [LESSONS](LESSONS.md) lesson 199); the teams do not change ends at
half-time ([GROUNDING](GROUNDING.md) L3); a keeper's acceleration fails the contract on 1 of 40 sweep seeds
(seed 8074, [issue #297](https://github.com/KLKH7/the-ninety-labs/issues/297)).
