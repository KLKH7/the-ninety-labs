## Budgets

- The Judge: 15 s per call in production, tried 2 times, on each of up to 3 drafts; then the claim is held (fail closed). Here 0.05 s.
- The whole Analyst to fact-checker workflow: 120 s in production (`live.AGENTS_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`, a design value); then no insight airs and the template cues stand. Here 2 s.
- The confidence gate: a claim citing a detection below 0.50 is held, and a template cue is drawn only for a detection at or above it.

Latencies are not measured here: every model is a stand-in, so a time would describe the stand-in. The live latencies belong to the deployed demo's traces.
