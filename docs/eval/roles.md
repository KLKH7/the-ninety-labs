(seeds 7 and 11 set the gates, 21 is held out). The engine's frames are spoiled as the tracker spoils
them: tracks broken after an exponential time, a persistent position error (0.587 m a side), 5%

assigned over each 15-minute window. Targets, fixed before the run: name 0.95, position
0.9, line 0.95, over at least 50 tracks.
