# Touches

Generated by `uv run python -m ninety.sim.touches` on seeds 40, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, 46, 47, 90 minutes each. A report beside published figures, never a target: nothing in the engine is tuned to these numbers. The comparisons are fixed in docs/EVALUATION.md ('Match engine: touches').

A **deliberate touch** is a player who has reacted to the ball playing it (foot, or above 0.45 m thigh, chest or head, shown here as *air*, or a keeper's hands). A **body hit** is the ball meeting a body that is not playing it. **Controlled** is the engine's verdict at the touch; **kept** is read off the frames afterwards: he was the next player to have the ball, within 10 s, before any stoppage. Speeds are the ball's relative to the player as it arrived.
