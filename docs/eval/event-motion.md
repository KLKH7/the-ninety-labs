For every event, the distance its reference object (the ball; the nearest player of the team for pressure and kick-off) moves between the event's frame and a frame early or late by the offset. This is why `ninety/eval/event_score.py` judges a detected spot at the detected time: judged at the true event's spot, a pass detected 0.4 s late would be metres off even if vision placed it perfectly.

The last column counts consecutive events of one type and team that are within 1.0 s of each other: how often place would be needed to tell two events apart.
