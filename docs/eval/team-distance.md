Reading: with every player tracked the distance is within a few percent of the truth (a track broken often loses its first and last step). A player missing from frames takes his distance with him, so the phone shows the tracked distance with its coverage, "from the footage shown", and does not scale it up. The demo's own tracker is to be graded the same way against its truth before the number is relied on.
