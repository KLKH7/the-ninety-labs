Reading: with every player tracked, the distance reads 0.936 to 1.122 times the truth per five minutes. The persistent position error walks, and its walk adds distance (more where players run less); a track broken often loses its first and last step. A player missing from frames takes his distance with him, so the phone shows the tracked distance with its coverage, "from the footage shown", and does not scale it up. The demo's own tracker is to be graded the same way against its truth before the number is relied on.
